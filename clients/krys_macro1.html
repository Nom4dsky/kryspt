Hybrid base — running, Olympic lifting, strength · 12 Oct 2026 – 3 Jan 2027

Macro 1 · Hybrid Base

Dates: 12 Oct 2026 – 3 Jan 2027  |  12 weeks
Target race: Jotunheimen Trail Run 73 km, July 2027

Build aerobic and structural base for January ultra prep, while banking strength and Olympic lifting quality before running volume forces lifting down to 1×/week. End state: ~60 km weeks, a 150 min long run, a first back-to-back, and strength held or higher.

Order of work

Follows Koop. Block 1 uphill power, Block 2 threshold, Block 3 steady state. Biggest load on most rested day (Saturday). Lifting follows NSCA logic: heaviest in general prep, trimmed as endurance volume rises.

Weekly Structure

  • Mon: Easy run + drills + strides, core, carries, mobility. Block 3: optional or off.
  • Tue: AM quality run (or Stairmaster in Block 1). PM Lift A, extensive plyo — at least 6 h gap.
  • Wed: Easy run + strides (+ drills in Blocks 1–2).
  • Thu: Lift B — intensive plyo, isometrics, technique and power, low fatigue.
  • Fri: Easy trail with a power-hike section.
  • Sat: Long run.
  • Sun: Rest. Weeks 10 and 11: easy B2B run.

Intensity Guide

  • Easy: RPE 3–4 (~5:50–6:30/km flat)
  • Hard reps: RPE 8–9, 5K effort
  • Tempo: RPE 6–7 (~4:50–5:05/km flat)
  • SSR: RPE 5–6 (~5:15–5:35/km flat, rolling or climbing)
  • Lifting: RPE 7 = 3 reps left · RPE 8 = 2 left · RPE 9 = 1 left
  • Squat start: ~105–110 kg if April's 160 kg is still your max — go by RPE

Warm-ups

  • Runs: 5–10 min easy before drills or strides
  • Quality runs: 15 min WU, 15 min CD
  • Lifts: Walking lunge 2×8/leg, diagonal lunge 1×6/leg, then empty-bar hang clean or snatch ×5. Ramp sets before first main lift.
  • Tue & Fri: Banded lateral walk 2×10 steps/side first
  • After Lift A: Static stretch hip extensors and abductors, 2×30s each
  • Session order: Warm-up → Plyometrics → Isometrics → Main lift → Accessories → Small muscles

Plyo & Isometrics

Plyometrics

  • Lift A (extensive): Low intensity, higher contacts, quick ankle work
  • Lift B (intensive): Box jumps, broad jumps, bounding, split jumps — fewer contacts, full recovery
  • Mon/Wed run drills = running technique, not plyo load
  • Soft or flat surface. Stop when contacts slow down.

Isometrics

  • After plyo, before main lift — RPE 6, never to failure
  • Lift A: Spanish squat hold + SL calf raise hold (from Block 2)
  • Lift B: Split squat hold + overhead squat bottom hold

Injury Prevention

  • Eccentric step-downs, calf raises, pogo and line hops, tibialis raises, hip abduction and SL work, core and carries, Monday prehab
  • Deloads every 4th week
  • Pain rule: 0–3/10 carry on · 4–5 modify · 6+ stop and reassess
  • Load spikes cause more overuse injuries than weak muscle
  • Introduce descents gradually — none big in Block 1
  • Shoes: rotate two pairs once weekly volume passes ~45 km
  • Low energy availability raises stress-injury risk

Progression & Load Control

  • Running: 10% a week or less outside deloads
  • Two sessions missing target RPE = repeat that week
  • Max 3 new things in one session

Log weekly

  • Stairmaster level and RPE or tempo splits
  • Load and RPE for clean, squat, front squat
  • Saturday leg feel
  • Any niggle (location, 0–10)

Mountain Days

  • Fri and Sat are flex slots — move long run to best-weather day
  • Plan by time and D+, not km
  • Weekly D+ may run up to ~20–25% over the table in good weather
  • Before a big descent day: light Lift B, skip step-downs
  • Next day after a big mountain day: easy or off
  • December in Oslo: ~6 h daylight — headlamp and microspikes
