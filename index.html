Meet your coach

Hi, I’m Przemek. People call me Krys.

I came to fitness the long way round: years of physically draining work, with sport always stubbornly in the background: cycling, martial arts, the gym. Eventually I got certified in the UK, moved to Norway, and started training people at SATS Sandvika.

That’s why I coach the way I do: calm, practical, and built for imperfect weeks. Most clients tell me the same thing after a few months: they feel stronger, the stairs don’t drain them, the trousers fit differently, the day is easier. That kind of change doesn’t make magazine covers. It tends to stick.

FAQ

Questions I hear a lot.

I’m a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Absolutely. I work with every level, from first-timers to people who’ve trained for years but feel stuck. Your plan is built from your experience, goals and daily life, never from a template.

How is online coaching different from in-person training?

Online coaching means an app with your plan and instructional videos, regular contact, and work on nutrition habits and tracking. In person, we train together at SATS Sandvika, ideal if you want someone beside you.

How quickly will I see results?

It depends on your goal. That’s an honest answer, not a deflection. Some changes show up in a month, others take six months or a year. I don’t promise shortcuts, because there aren’t any. I promise a plan that moves you in the right direction.

Can I train if I have an injury or pain?

Almost always, yes. An injury doesn’t automatically mean stopping. It means choosing exercises more cleverly. Sometimes it’s even a chance to work on something you’ve been neglecting.

Do you help with nutrition?

Yes, within my area of competence: reviewing calorie balance and eating habits. I don’t write full diet plans. I’m not a dietitian and don’t pretend to be. If you need deeper work, I’ll point you to a trusted specialist.

How often will we be in contact?

I aim to reply within a few hours, Monday to Saturday. I don’t disappear after sending the plan.

Can you help with a race or event goal, like a 5k or HYROX?

Yes. I compete in HYROX myself and understand what race prep demands: the balance of strength, functional fitness and conditioning. Whether it’s your first finish or a sub-70-minute time, we can build around it.
