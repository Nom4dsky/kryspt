Privacy notice

How I handle your personal data when you use kryspt.com or get in touch with me.

Who is responsible

Przemyslaw Krys, trading as KRYS PT, personal trainer at SATS Sandvika Panorama, Norway. You can reach me through the contact form on this site or on WhatsApp.

What I collect

Only what you send me: your name, email address, the type of training you are interested in, your main goal and any message you add in the contact form. If you message me on WhatsApp, I see your number and your messages.

Why and on what basis

To reply to your enquiry and, if you want, to arrange a consultation or coaching. This is based on your request to be contacted and on steps taken before a possible agreement (GDPR Article 6(1)(b) and (a)). If we start working together, I may ask you for health information such as injuries. I only collect that with your explicit consent and only to plan safe training.

Who else processes it

The contact form is handled by Web3Forms, which passes your message to my inbox. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which keeps technical logs such as IP addresses. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is sent to Google when the page loads. I do not sell your data and I do not use it for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use advertising cookies or analytics. It stores your language choice (EN or PL) in your own browser so the page remembers it. That choice never leaves your device.

How long I keep it

Enquiries are kept only as long as needed to answer you. If we work together, your details are kept for the length of the coaching and as long as I am required to keep records. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to take it with you. If you are unhappy with how I handle your data, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).
